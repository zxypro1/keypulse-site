常见问题与技术支持 (FAQ)

1. 为什么需要「输入监控 (Input Monitoring)」权限？
macOS 要求任何在后台统计全局按键次数的工具必须获得用户授权。键律仅读取按键事件的时间戳与四种抽象类型（普通输入、退格、方向/回车、快捷键），绝不读取具体输入的文字内容。

2. 启动后在哪里打开主界面？
键律是纯菜单栏工具（无 Dock 图标），启动后点击屏幕右上角菜单栏中的状态表情图标即可展开完整看板。

Frequently Asked Questions & Support

1. Why does KeyPulse request Input Monitoring permission?
macOS requires Input Monitoring permission to count background keystrokes across apps. KeyPulse only records timestamps across four generic categories (Typing, Backspace, Navigation/Enter, Shortcuts) and never inspects what you type.

2. Where do I find KeyPulse after launching?
KeyPulse is a clean menu bar utility (no Dock clutter). Click the status emoji icon in the top-right macOS menu bar to open the dashboard.
