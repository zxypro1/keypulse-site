键律 (KeyPulse) 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-11-09

1. 核心承诺：我们不收集任何个人数据

键律 (KeyPulse) 是一款纯本地沙盒运行的 macOS 菜单栏工具。本应用不包含任何网络请求代码、不连接任何远程服务器、不接入任何第三方统计或广告 SDK。您的所有统计数据 100% 仅保存在您本机的应用沙盒目录中。

2. 关于「输入监控 (Input Monitoring)」权限的使用说明

  • 仅统计按键节奏与分类计数：为了计算实时打字速度（键/分）、退格修改率与连续专注时长，应用仅将按键事件归类为四种抽象类型（普通输入、退格删除、方向/回车、快捷键）并记录时间间隔。
  • 绝不读取或记录字符内容：应用在代码层面从不读取您输入的具体字母、文字、代码或密码内容（Zero Keylogging）。
  • 随时可暂停与清除：您可以随时在应用面板右上角一键暂停监测，或在底部点击「重置」彻底清空本机今日统计数据。

100% On-Device · Zero Data Collection

KeyPulse Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection & Zero Network Access

KeyPulse operates 100% locally inside the macOS App Sandbox on your Mac. The application contains no networking code, no cloud sync, no analytics trackers, and no third-party SDKs. No personal or usage data ever leaves your device.

2. How Input Monitoring Permission Is Used

  • Timing & Generic Counts Only: To evaluate your typing cadence, backspace ratio, and continuous focus duration, KeyPulse only measures timestamps across four generic keystroke categories (Typing, Backspace, Navigation/Enter, Shortcuts).
  • Never Logs Characters or Passwords: KeyPulse never inspects, reads, or stores the characters, words, code, or passwords you type (Zero Keylogging).
  • Full User Control: You can pause monitoring at any time with a single click or reset all local statistics from the bottom bar of the app.
